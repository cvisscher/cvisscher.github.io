READING THE DIAGRAM

The world of substellar objects

A number of distinct regimes can be seen in the substellar population: terrestrial worlds, ice giants and gas giants, and brown dwarfs. Brown dwarfs, which lack sustained core fusion, show a near-constant radius trend caused by increasing gravitational compression; stellar objects demonstrate increasing radii due to hydrostatic equilibrium with core radiation pressure
