Data and methods

Planck TT data show how temperature variation depends on angular scale. The dots use individual multipoles below ℓ = 30 and bandpowers above, with published uncertainty bars. Dℓ = ℓ(ℓ + 1)Cℓ/(2π). Planck public data release 3

The model line interpolates a grid of lensed CAMB spectra with fixed primordial tilt (ns = 0.9649), amplitude (As = 2.1 × 10−9), optical depth (τ = 0.0544), and neutrino mass (0.06 eV). The H₀ fit minimizes squared residuals weighted by the quoted errors for 50 ≤ ℓ ≤ 2000, holding the ordinary matter and dark energy fractions fixed. It uses model values at band centers and ignores covariance. Treat it as an educational estimate. CAMB

The redshift calculator integrates the flat ΛCDM expansion history using the fitted H₀ and the corresponding matter and dark energy densities. It includes photons and relativistic light neutrinos, with the 0.06 eV neutrino treated as matter. Distance and time formulas
